# University Commons Level 1

`map.json` is a vector trace of the supplied Level 1 wayfinding and first-floor evacuation diagrams. The wayfinding sign faces west at the bottom, with north to the left. The evacuation diagram provided a second view of the atrium, stair cores, and curved south rooms. The outline represents the first floor and includes schematic walls and public approaches. Other floors and room-level subdivisions within unlabelled suites are outside this plan's coverage.

Room numbers follow the posted sign. The university and Students' Union also identify the Decima Robinson Support Centre, InfoLink, Daily Grind, and Campus Tours in University Commons. [Decima Robinson Support Centre](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/mathematical-and-statistical-sciences/undergraduate-studies/decima-support-centre/index.html) · [InfoLink](https://www.su.ualberta.ca/these-are-your-resources/these-are-your-learning-supports/infolink/) · [Daily Grind](https://webadmin.su.ualberta.ca/this-is-your-space/these-are-your-businesses/daily-grind/) · [Campus tours](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/admissions/events/open-house/index.html)

The geographic projection is approximate. The three campus connections use mapped Level 1 doors on the east and south sides. The historic central south door is mapped on Level 2. Current operating hours and building access are published by the university.
